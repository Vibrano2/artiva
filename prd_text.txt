Executive SummaryArtiva is a mobile-responsive web platform that connects homeowners and small contractors in Abuja with verified, available artisans for urgent home repairs. It addresses a two-sided market failure: clients cannot find a reliable artisan fast enough during an emergency, and skilled artisans cannot get consistent, well-paying leads.The platform&apos;s core value proposition is speed and protection: a verified artisan is matched in under 30 minutes, with the client&apos;s payment held in escrow until the job is confirmed complete. It is not a directory — it is a trust-and-urgency marketplace. All negotiation, messaging, and transactions happen inside the app; there is no external WhatsApp or phone hand-off.This PRD covers the MVP scope only: the minimum set of features required to prove the core loop end-to-end, generate real revenue, and demonstrate Product-Market Fit within a single beachhead estate — Life Camp (NTA Estate), Abuja.As of v1.9, the client&apos;s platform fee and job value are collected in a single consolidated checkout rather than two separate payments, and — for jobs that require materials — the artisan never handles client cash for supplies. Instead, the artisan submits a supplier proforma invoice and the platform pays the supplier directly from the escrowed funds. Both changes reduce friction and reduce fraud risk without changing the underlying revenue model.MetricTargetQ1 Revenue (conservative)₦230,000Q1 Revenue (optimistic)₦362,500Revenue target (hackathon brief)₦200,000Beachhead launch siteLife Camp (NTA Estate), AbujaPhase 2 expansionKaru, Mararaba (both NTA Properties estates)Seed artisans at launch30 across 6 tradesClient match fee₦500 per matchCommission on jobs10% of job value, held in escrow at bookingMVP timeline4 weeksPrimary researchArtisans (n=47), Customers (n=105)2.
